## Brand & Style

This design system embodies "Editorial Celebration Intelligence"—an aesthetic synergy of high-end wedding editorial spreads, bespoke luxury hospitality collateral, and high-velocity workflow architecture. The experience targets modern event hosts, master planners, creative directors, and couples who require both exacting logistical precision and exquisite aesthetic taste. 

The emotional signature is poised, romantic, and reassuringly authoritative:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Rooted in tactile, physical paper textures, warm alabaster foundations, and bespoke typography rather than sterile tech-minimalism.
- **Disciplined Editorial Layout:** Characterized by deliberate asymmetric pacing, high typographic contrast, hairline frames, and museum-grade whitespace.
- **Invisible Intelligence:** AI interactions avoid cold robotic metaphors, manifesting instead as an intuitive private concierge characterized by quiet recommendations, gold-leaf shimmer states, and refined four-point sparkle celebration emblems.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from wedding invitations printed on deckled cotton paper, aged champagne wax seals, pressed garden roses, and rich archival ink.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#392538`):** Deep Plum / Archival Ink. Replaces pure black for all structural typography, primary buttons, and focal elements, lending warmth and high-contrast editorial legibility.
- **Secondary (`#C5A46D`):** Muted Champagne Gold. Used for celebration badges, verified markers, AI prompt accents, focus rings, and premium milestone highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#C97862`):** Soft Terracotta / Vintage Rose. Reserved for emotional highlights, guest response indicators, and secondary accent interactive states.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F0`):** Ivory / Warm Paper. Sets the ambient tone of the entire system, preventing sterile digital glare while maintaining natural daylight softness.

### Functional & State Roles
- **Card Base Surface:** `#FFFFFF` on `#FAF7F0` page ground; nested elevated cards use `#F5F0E8`.
- **Structural Borders & Separators:** `#E8E1D5` for primary hairline framing (1px); `#DFD7C7` for interactive borders and subtle input dividers.
- **Success / Botanical Sage:** `#4A6B53` signifies confirmed RSVPs, locked vendor deposits, and completed timeline phases.
- **Warning / Spiced Ochre:** `#D97706` alerts to budget overruns or pending vendor deadlines.

## Typography

Typographic scale is balanced between the literary gravitas of `Playfair Display` and the functional clarity of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Editorial Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for page headings, event titles, narrative studio summaries, and AI-generated vision briefs. Maintain tight negative letter-spacing on display scales to preserve editorial density.
- **Productivity & Interface Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Powers all dashboard data grids, guest seating manifests, vendor budget inputs, and system navigation. Neutral geometric shapes offset the display serif's ornamentation.
- **Eyebrow & Metadata Accents:** Render `label-xs` in uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`) when introducing card categories, chronological timelines, or curation status badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors modern luxury editorial spreads with fluid structural alignment. 

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max width `1440px`), collapsing to 8 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile. 
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Generous margins create breathing room around data-intensive tasks. Large editorial hero sections leverage `space-2xl` padding to distinguish planning milestones from dense administrative tools.
- **Reflow Rules:** Complex multi-column seating and mood-board visualizers stack gracefully on mobile viewport widths, shifting horizontal sub-navigation into sticky horizontal carousels.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of editorial layering, warm tonal contrasts, and tactile hairline boundaries.

- **Hairline Framing:** Primary depth is established using 1px solid borders (`#E8E1D5`). Cards, modal dialogs, and segmented toolbars frame content cleanly without digital heaviness.
- **Tonal Layering:** 
  - Level 0 (Canvas): Ivory `#FAF7F0`.
  - Level 1 (Panels & Master Cards): `#FFFFFF` bounded by `#E8E1D5`.
  - Level 2 (Nested Tooling / AI Recommendation Blocks): `#F5F0E8` with no shadow, retaining flat, pressed-paper physicality.
- **Ambient Illumination:** When floating action bars, dropdown menus, or modal sheets require z-axis lift, use an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with Deep Plum:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(57, 37, 56, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(57, 37, 56, 0.03);`
- **Celebratory Glass:** Sticky timeline controllers and header navigation use frosted paper translucency (`rgba(250, 247, 240, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a bottom border of `#E8E1D5`).

## Shapes

The shape system balances architectural order with tactile softness.
- **Standard Radius (`rounded` = 0.5rem):** Form inputs, tooltips, list row selections, and default action buttons.
- **Card & Container Radius (`rounded-lg` = 1rem, `rounded-xl` = 1.5rem):** Guest profile cards, inspiration canvas tiles, and AI generation preview windows.
- **Pill Geometry:** Reserved strictly for metadata tags, RSVP status badges, and quick-filter pills (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Four-Point Sparkle Motif:** Geometric decorative accents and AI prompt triggers adopt a stylized 4-point star icon inscribed within a champagne-tinted circular or square silhouette.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Plum background (`#392538`), text `#FAF7F0`, font `label-lg`, height 44px, `rounded` (0.5rem). Hover shifts background to `#252321` with subtle 1px uplift.
- **Secondary / Studio Outline:** Transparent background, 1px border `#392538`, text `#392538`. Hover state fills with warm ivory-toned tint `rgba(57, 37, 56, 0.04)`.
- **AI Concierge Action:** Linear gradient background (`#C5A46D` to `#D4AF37`), Deep Plum text `#252321`, paired with the 4-point sparkle icon prefix.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Height 24px, 12px horizontal padding, pill-shaped.
  - *Confirmed / Accepted:* Background `rgba(74, 107, 83, 0.1)`, text `#4A6B53`, 1px border `rgba(74, 107, 83, 0.2)`.
  - *Curated / AI Suggestion:* Background `rgba(197, 164, 109, 0.12)`, text `#93723E`, 1px border `rgba(197, 164, 109, 0.3)`.
  - *Pending / RSVP Awaited:* Background `rgba(201, 120, 98, 0.1)`, text `#C97862`, 1px border `rgba(201, 120, 98, 0.2)`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Style:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#DFD7C7`, height 44px, horizontal padding `space-md`, text `#392538`, font `body-md`, radius `0.5rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#C5A46D` with a subtle box-shadow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 164, 109, 0.15)`. No harsh browser outlines.
- **Floating Labels:** Rendered in `label-xs` uppercase tracking in muted ink (`rgba(57, 37, 56, 0.6)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** 18px square, `rounded` 4px, border 1.5px solid `#DFD7C7`. When checked, background fills with `#392538` displaying a white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** 18px circle, border 1.5px solid `#DFD7C7`. When selected, reveals an inner concentric dot in `#C5A46D`.

### Cards & Editorial Canvas
- **Editorial Canvas Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px hairline border `#E8E1D5`, `rounded-xl` (1.5rem), internal padding `space-xl`.
- **Interactive Moodboard / Seating Tile:** Outer border `#E8E1D5`, internal radius `1rem`, smooth scale hover (`transform: translateY(-2px)` with shadow elevation transition).

### Studio Timeline & Milestone Trackers
- Continuous vertical or horizontal hairline (#E8E1D5) punctuated by circular node indicators: completed nodes in `#C5A46D` with checkmark; active step in `#392538` with outer champagne ring.
